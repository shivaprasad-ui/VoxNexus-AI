Speak. Verify. Trust.

A trust layer for voice calls. While a customer talks, we check that the voice is real, that it is the right person, and whether the call sounds like a scam, before anything sensitive is allowed.

Locked session→ Live call check→ One trust score→ Challenge sentence if trust drops→ One-time transfer approval

1. Locked by default

Every session starts unverified with trust 0. Nothing sensitive is allowed.

2. Real voice, right person

Aletheia checks the voice is not AI-generated. Eidos checks it matches the registered person.

3. Listens to the call too

Call Guard reads what is said. Pressure, urgency and requests for an OTP lower the same trust score.

Who it helps

Bank customers

Especially older people targeted by voice-clone and scam calls.

Call-centre staff

A quick, clear check before they act on a spoken request.

Fraud and compliance teams

A log of every check and the reason for every block.
